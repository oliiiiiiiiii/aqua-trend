# Task 09b — Sea-ice concentration before/after maps and per-pixel trend

**Data:** NSIDC G02135 v4 monthly concentration GeoTIFFs (25 km, polar stereographic), September (Arctic minimum), February & September (Antarctic min/max), 1979–2026.

## Results
- Arctic September: ice area lost ≈ **2.45 million km²** between 1979–88 and 2017–26 averages (≈ 68× the area of Taiwan).
- 77 % of the Arctic pixels that ever carry September ice have a significant (FDR) negative concentration trend.
- Antarctic: −0.29 M km² (Feb) and −0.54 M km² (Sep) between the same periods — loss concentrated after 2016 (see task 09).
