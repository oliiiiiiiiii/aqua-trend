# Task 37 — East Asia water extremes from space

**Method.**
- GRACE TWS area means (land only), deseasonalised but not detrended, ranked among all months since 2002.
- IMERG 0.1° rain anomalies vs the 2001–2025 normal of each calendar month.
- North China 2021 is ranked by its 3-month storage gain, because the long-term depletion trend hides the absolute level.
- Three Gorges: storage in the reservoir region (106–111.5°E, 29–31.5°N) minus the upstream Sichuan basin, compared across impoundment stages, plus a Pettitt test.

**Results.**

| Event | GRACE | Rain |
|---|---|---|

| North China 2021 | +13.6 cm in 3 months from 2021-07, #2 of 231 | Henan July 247%, #1 wettest |
| Pearl / South China 2021 | -8.6 cm (2021-06), #11 | — |
| Taiwan 2021 | (GRACE too coarse) | Jan–May 60%, #2 driest |

Three Gorges stage means (cm): before 2003-06: +0.28, 2003-06..2006-09: +0.53, 2006-10..2008-10: +2.17, 2008-11..2010-09: +4.89, 2010-10..2015: -0.06. The result is inconclusive; see FINDINGS W18.
