**Mass conservation closes within 28 Gt/yr (4 %).**
Altimetry GMSL 2002–2026: 3.47 mm/yr = ocean mass 2.02 + **thermal expansion ≈ 1.45 mm/yr** (Argo-based estimates ≈ 1.3 mm/yr ✔).

**Story:** every drop of water lost from aquifers in NW India, the Middle East or California (tasks 01/21) ends up in the ocean — human groundwater use adds ≈ 0.3 mm/yr to sea-level rise, about as much as all of Antarctica.
